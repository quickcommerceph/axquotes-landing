# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js 16 App Router, React 19, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS v4, based on the conventions of `quickcommerce/murple-web`.

## Users

New retail traders who need to understand the offer quickly, explore available markets, and feel confident enough to create an account.

## Product Purpose

Axquotes presents global CFD market access, platform tools, learning resources, and trader benefits in a clear conversion-focused landing page.

## Positioning

One visually clear trading platform that combines broad market access, practical tools, and rewards without overwhelming a newer trader.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The website is a marketing surface, not a live trading application.
- CTA destinations are section anchors or placeholders until production URLs are supplied.
- No authentication, trading API, or account backend is part of this build.
- Only claims and figures present in the supplied source may be shown.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: Axquotes.
- Preserve the supplied Axquotes logo and its blue, coral, white, and black identity.
- Preserve the current light editorial foundation, bold typography, and trading-terminal visual motif.
- eToro is a reference for page structure and interaction hierarchy only, never for assets, copy, claims, or branding.

## Product Principles

- Make global markets approachable without flattening the product’s professional character.
- Demonstrate the platform visually before asking visitors to trust abstract claims.
- Use clear, repeated actions and keep risk disclosure visible.
- Prefer proof and useful detail over decorative marketing chrome.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic landmarks, reduced motion, responsive layouts, and WCAG AA contrast.
